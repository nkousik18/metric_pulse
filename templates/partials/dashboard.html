Why did the metric move?

MetricPulse watches a daily business metric, flags the days that break pattern, and explains them: which segments drove the change, a plain-English summary, and an AI investigation whose every claim is checked against the numbers. Any action it proposes waits for a person to approve it.

  1. Detectz-score and data-freshness checks on every day
  2. Decomposethe change split by region, product and payment
  3. Explainan LLM agent writes the cause and cites the numbers
  4. Actan agent proposes a response; a person approves it

Data: Olist Brazilian e-commerce, 451,535 rows, 2016–2018. Each agent is scored by its own eval suite; the scores are under . Built end to end by Kousik Nandury.
